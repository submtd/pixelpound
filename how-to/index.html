How-to videos

Short videos of each task in the app, step by step. Each one runs under a minute.

Meet your first pup

Video 1
  1. Connect your wallet. Tap Select Wallet, choose your wallet, then approve in the wallet app.
  2. Pick your price. Tap + or type an amount. Every rehome amount scales with the price you choose. Then tap Meet a pup and confirm in your wallet.
  3. Adopt or rehome?. You have two minutes: rehome your pup for a guaranteed amount in USDC, or adopt it as an NFT.

Find your pups

Video 2
  1. Open My Pups. Tap My Pups. Every pup you have adopted is here, with its name, tier and traits.
  2. Finish with a pup you left. A pup you left before deciding waits here. Tap Open it on the Pound page, then adopt it or rehome it.

How traits and tiers work

Video 3
  1. Seven trait layers. Every pup rolls one trait per layer: background, coat, ears, eyes, mouth, neckwear and headwear.
  2. Points add up to a score. Rarer traits carry more points. Your pup's score is the total across all seven layers.
  3. Your score sets the tier. The tier table shows the score each tier needs and how often it comes up.

Stake USDC

Video 4
  1. Know the risk. Stakers back every rehome. You can lose money: stakers pay out when players pull big tiers.
  2. Deposit USDC. Enter an amount, tick the box, tap Deposit and approve in your wallet.
  3. Check your stake. Your stake shows at the top of the page and moves with the pool.

Withdraw your stake

Video 5
  1. Request a withdrawal. Enter an amount and tap Request. Requests are final.
  2. Lock and claim. When open pups are done, tap Lock and claim. One approval locks your amount and pays it to your wallet.
